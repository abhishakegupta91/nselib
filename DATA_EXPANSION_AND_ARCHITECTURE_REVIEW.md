## Part 2 — Code Quality & Architecture Review

### Rating: **5.5 / 10 — functional but structurally uneven**

The 20% of the library that `tb-collector` actually depends on (`capital_market`, `derivatives`, `indices` core functions) is solid: real HTTP retry/backoff, real trading-calendar-aware date resolution, real auto-pagination, consistent custom exception types. What drags the score down is almost entirely in the *other* 80% — modules nobody uses, near-zero tests, and a few convention violations sitting in exactly the places the library's own `CLAUDE.md` says shouldn't have them.

### What's genuinely good

- **Real retry/backoff**, not just documented aspiration: `request_maker.py:29-84` — `urllib3.Retry(total=3, backoff_factor=1, status_forcelist=[429,500,502,503,504])` plus a self-healing "clear cookies and retry once on 401/403" path. This contradicts the impression `libs/nselib/CLAUDE.md`'s "Rate limiting: NSE blocks aggressive scrapers... add delays between batch calls in callers" gives — the delay/backoff *is* handled centrally; only inter-call spacing across a batch is left to callers, which is reasonable.
- **Real trading-calendar awareness**, not naive calendar-day math: `libutil.py:104-135` (`derive_from_and_to_date`) walks backward day-by-day against `pandas_market_calendars.get_calendar("NSE")` until it lands on an actual trading day.
- **Genuine auto-pagination** in both `capital_market/api.py:35-61` and `derivatives/derivative_data.py:31-73`, correctly sized to each API's real per-call limit (365d vs 90d) — not a stub.
- **Consistent custom exception hierarchy** (`errors.py`) used fairly uniformly across `capital_market` and `derivatives`.

### Concrete findings

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A1 | **Version mismatch** — `nselib/__init__.py:9` says `__version__ = "3.1.4"`, `setup.py:8` says `version='3.1.5'`. Anyone pinning or logging `nselib.__version__` gets the wrong number. | `nselib/__init__.py:9` vs `setup.py:8` | Low, trivial fix |
| A2 | **Dead/misleading conditional** — `"VAR_COLUMNS" in globals()` in `get_var_data` always evaluates `False` in `network.py`'s namespace (`VAR_COLUMNS` is only ever imported into `api.py`), so this always takes the `header=None` branch. | A3 | **Direct `requests.get()` bypassing the shared session/retry/cookie layer**, inside a per-row loop with no batching or backoff — violates the library's own stated convention ("All HTTP: encapsulated in `CapitalMarketHelper`... | A5 | **`nsdl_fpi.py` is a severe architectural outlier** — ~590 lines, the single heaviest file in the library. | A8 | **Duplicated pagination logic** — `capital_market/api.py:35-61` and `derivatives/derivative_data.py:31-73` are near-identical re-implementations of the same date-chunking loop, differing only in default `chunk_days` (365 vs 90) and a `<=`/`<` boundary. | A9 | **Duplicated business-growth normalization helpers** — `_normalize_business_growth_cm_segment_financial_year` / `_normalize_business_growth_cm_segment_daily_args` (`capital_market/api.py:1128-1177`) are near-verbatim copies of `_normalize_business_growth_fo_segment_financial_year` / `_normalize_business_growth_fo_segment_daily_args` (`derivatives/derivative_data.py:768-797`). | A10 | **Near-zero test coverage.** `tests/` contains exactly one file, `test_imports.py` — 19 lines, and its entire job is to recursively `importlib.import_module` every submodule and assert it's not `None`. | A12 | **Inconsistent docstring style** — Sphinx-style (`:param x:`) throughout most of `capital_market/api.py`, Google-style (`Args:`/`Returns:`) in `derivatives/derivative_data.py` and the newer `indices/index_data.py`.
